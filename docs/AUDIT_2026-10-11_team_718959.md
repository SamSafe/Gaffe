## Requirements for further improvement

1. **Before the GW7 decision:** ingest finalized GW6, refresh bootstrap and odds, verify João Pedro’s role/minutes and Schade’s fitness, then rerun. GW7 closes at 04:00 Edmonton on 17 October. Use a per-GW manual minutes override only when supported by current team news. The biggest unresolved team decision is minutes, not a need to spend a transfer hit.
2. **Strict model evaluation:** train-only chronological stopping is implemented in the follow-up, including core evaluators, live prediction and diagnostics. Minutes and goals/assists components were reevaluated on seasons 24 and 25. Regenerate complete joint-xPts and team backtests next. Archived MAE, calibration and rank claims remain exploratory evidence; this repair alone does not correct closing-odds look-ahead or season-specific metadata. No full historical team backtest was regenerated here.
3. **Player-level live calibration:** retain pre-deadline forecasts and score them only after gameweek finalization. Measure minutes log loss, goal/assist deviance, xPts bias/MAE, captain outcomes and lineup regret by position and starting status. Historical aggregate MAE includes many non-playing rows and can conceal weak prediction on the expensive players the team needs.
4. **Better future-fixture inputs:** collect odds when later GWs are priced, distinguish market defaults from real fixture strength, and validate how frozen rolling form and projected rest behave over longer horizons. GW9–12 should have lower influence/confidence until their inputs improve.
5. **Bonus and defensive-contribution calibration:** validate 2026/27 bonus residuals against observed results, add player-specific save/card rates only if they beat shared priors out of sample, and handle defensive-contribution triggers per fixture in double gameweeks. Appearance scaling fixes DNP inflation but does not prove the current shrinkage weights are optimal.
6. **State and chip planning:** update bank/FT/cost basis after every actual transfer, or restore private read-only account sync. The current fixture-only scheduler has no Free Hit date when no blank gameweek is known; ensure your unused FH1 is explicitly reconsidered before its GW19 expiry. First-half BB/TC/WC are already spent.
7. **Historical validity:** repeat research comparisons with season-specific chip rules, odds available before deadlines, season-specific player positions and code/data-versioned caches. Keep the existing suspension/news handling but verify bans against current fixtures, particularly blanks/doubles and cup-served bans.

These changes repair concrete serving, accounting and evaluation errors. They do not establish a new accuracy gain or a reliable end-of-season rank forecast.
