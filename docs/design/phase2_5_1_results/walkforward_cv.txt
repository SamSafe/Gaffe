Net assessment:
- MAE: marginally worse on 3 of 4 folds (0.001-0.007 absolute, 0.1-0.8% rel).
- Brier(≥6): essentially unchanged.
- Captaincy: mixed — better on 21/22 (+70), 22/23 (+12), 24/25 (+8); worse
  on 23/24 (-104). All folds still pass the gate (sim > base).
- Calibration: 24/25 improved 9/10 → 10/10 deciles.

This is a CORRECTNESS fix — the pre-multinomial sampler had structurally
wrong joint outcomes (Σ player_goals could exceed team_score). The
multinomial enforces Σ player_goals = team_score by construction, with
the side effect of trimming inflated tails on a few players' bonus mass.
The slight MAE regression and 23/24 captaincy regression reflect the loss
of "lucky" tail picks that wouldn't have generalized.
