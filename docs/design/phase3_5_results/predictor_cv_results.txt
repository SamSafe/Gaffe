Diagnosis (why the predictor doesn't beat naive base-rate):
The transfers_in/out signal at GW-end is too late to be predictive — by
the time we observe it, FPL's price-change algorithm has already locked
in (or not) the boundary movement. With richer intra-week transfer
dynamics (current-day net transfers, time-decayed signals, transfer-
threshold-aware features) the predictor could improve. Our GW-end-
snapshot data isn't enough.

v2 candidate features:
- Intra-GW snapshots (every 6 hours from FPL bootstrap-static)
- transfers_in_pct = transfers_in / selected (rate-of-change vs ownership)
- Days-since-last-price-change per player
- Cross-over signals (player gone from -threshold to +threshold)
